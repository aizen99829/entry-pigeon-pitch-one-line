# Pigeon Pitch, One Line

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT -->

A make.fun bounty entry: one line, one pigeon, one elevator pitch
delivered to a room full of investors.

## The pitch

> Coo. My startup delivers premium breadcrumbs to every rooftop in the city — we're pre-seed, pre-revenue, and already pecking at a total addressable market of one billion park benches.

The bird opens with a coo, then sells the dream the way every founder
does: big market, bold claims, zero revenue. The only difference is
that this founder's entire business model is crumbs, and its total
addressable market is every park bench in the country.

## How it works

`main.py` holds the single pitch line, validates that it is exactly
one line of at most 200 characters, and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The script prints the pitch and writes the same line to the output
file, so the entry the grader reads is exactly the line above.
